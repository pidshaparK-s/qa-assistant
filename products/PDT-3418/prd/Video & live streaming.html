A1: 🤔 Debt ticket — Video & live streaming: tapping anywhere pauses playback

What's broken or missing

Tapping anywhere on the video or live stream player surface triggers a pause, regardless of where on screen the user taps. This conflicts with the standard consumer video player convention established by YouTube, TikTok, Facebook — where pause is triggered by a dedicated tap target, and other screen areas either reveal controls or do nothing. The current behavior means users accidentally pause video and live streams constantly during normal viewing. For live streams specifically, an accidental pause is especially disruptive, so resuming drops the viewer mid-stream.

Who experiences it and how often

Affects every customer using the video player bundled in our UIKit. Any customer who’s actively using live streaming or video feeds as engagement surfaces would be disproportionately exposed.

Directional solution space

  • Tapping the player surface should not trigger pause by default
  • Pause/play toggle should be bound to a dedicated tap target: either a central button zone (standard YouTube/Instagram convention) or a double-tap gesture — Design to recommend

Scope

  • Applies to video & live stream posts in global, user, community, and event feeds, as well as media galleries on UIKit (all platforms inclusive of React Native & Flutter)
  • Does not apply to Stories / Clips

Known constraints or dependencies

None known at the moment

A2: 📝 Product Specs

Release Note

Tapping a video or live stream in your app no longer accidentally pauses it. Your users can now tap the player freely, controls appear on tap, and playback only pauses when they deliberately hit the pause button. This means fewer disrupted viewing sessions and a smoother experience for anyone watching live streams or video content in your community.

Scope

  • Platforms supported:
    • UIKit iOS
    • UIKit Android
    • UIKit React Native
    • UIKit Flutter
    • WebUIKIT
  • Platform not supported
    • Desktop
  • Functionality in scope:
    • Tap on player surface reveals controls overlay; pause/play bound to central button; applies to video posts and live streams across all in-scope feed surfaces and media gallery:
      • Event discussion feed
      • Global Feed
      • Community
      • Gallery (Video)
    • Nice to have: Skip back 10 seconds and skip forward 10 seconds buttons in the controls overlay — useful for video posts but not critical to the core gesture fix. Can be descoped if it impacts delivery timeline.
  • Functionality that is out of scope:
    • This behavior will not apply to clips and stories

User Stories

UC1: As a community member watching a video or live stream, I want tapping the player surface to reveal controls rather than toggle pause, so that I don't accidentally interrupt playback during normal viewing.

Preconditions

  • User is viewing a video post or live stream post on any scoped surface

Entry points

  • A. Video or live stream is playing inline in any feed (global, user, community, event discussion)
  • B. Video or live stream is open in a media gallery or fullscreen view

Acceptance criteria

Tap behaviour

  • Given a video or live stream is playing, when the user taps anywhere on the player surface
    • Then the controls overlay appears
    • And playback continues uninterrupted — the video or stream does not pause
  • Given the controls overlay is visible, when the user taps anywhere on the player surface outside the central pause/play button
    • Then the overlay dismisses
    • And playback continues uninterrupted
  • Given the controls overlay is visible, when the user takes no further action
    • Then the overlay auto-dismisses after a short timeout ( 1 second)
    • And playback continues (confirm auto-dismiss timeout with eng — assume 3s)

Pause/play button

  • Given a video or live stream is playing and the controls overlay is visible, when the user taps the central pause/play button
    • Then playback pauses
    • And the button icon switches from pause to play
  • Given a video or live stream is paused, when the user taps the central play button (controls visible, or re-summoned by a tap)
    • Then playback resumes from the exact position at which it was paused
    • And the button icon switches from play to pause

Platform and surface coverage

  • Given any in-scope surface (global feed, user feed, community feed, event discussion feed, media gallery), when the user taps the player surface
    • Then the behaviour described above is identical regardless of surface

UC2: As a community member watching a live stream, I want to know when I'm paused and behind the live edge, so that I understand my viewing context and can choose to resume or catch up.

Preconditions

  • User is watching a live stream with Room status: live

Entry points

  • A. User deliberately pauses the live stream via the central pause button

Acceptance criteria

Behind-live indicator

  • Given the user pauses a live stream, when the pause is applied
    • Then a visual indicator is shown communicating the stream is paused and the viewer is behind the live edge
    • And the indicator is visible without requiring an additional tap
  • Given the user resumes from a paused live stream, when playback restarts
    • Then playback resumes from the position at which it was paused — not from the live edge (confirm: is a "return to live" CTA in scope for this ticket or a follow-on?)

Stream ends while viewer is paused

  • Given the user has paused a live stream, when the Room transitions to ended or recorded during the pause
    • Then the player moves to an appropriate ended or recorded state
    • And the viewer is not left on a frozen frame with no feedback

NICE TO HAVE
UC3: As a community member watching a video, I want to skip backward or forward 10 seconds so that I can quickly rewatch or jump ahead without scrubbing.

Preconditions

  • User is viewing a video post (not a live stream)
  • The controls overlay is visible

Entry points

  • A. User taps the player surface to reveal the controls overlay
  • B. Controls overlay is already visible

Acceptance criteria

Skip controls

  • Given the controls overlay is visible, when the user taps the skip back button (−10s)
    • Then playback jumps back 10 seconds from the current position
    • And playback continues without interruption
  • Given the controls overlay is visible, when the user taps the skip forward button (+10s)
    • Then playback jumps forward 10 seconds from the current position
    • And playback continues without interruption

Boundary behaviour

  • Given the user taps skip back and the current position is less than 10 seconds into the video, when the action is applied
    • Then playback jumps to 0:00 — it does not go to a negative position
  • Given the user taps skip forward and the current position is within 10 seconds of the video end, when the action is applied
    • Then playback jumps to the final frame and enters the end state — it does not loop or error

Skip controls and pause state

  • Given the video is paused and the controls overlay is visible, when the user taps either skip button
    • Then the position updates accordingly
    • And the video remains paused — skip does not auto-resume playback

Live streams

  • Given a live stream is playing, when the controls overlay is visible
    • Then the skip back and skip forward buttons are not shown — seeking is not applicable on a live stream
